About Cleaning Geniee

We started in 2016 with two people, one van and a simple frustration: cleaning services that showed up late, cut corners and disappeared when something went wrong.

Today we're a team of 40 trained cleaners serving homes, offices, showrooms and newly handed-over apartments. Every cleaner is police-verified and trained in-house on surface safety — what's right for Italian marble is wrong for engineered wood, and knowing the difference is the job.

We use pH-balanced, child- and pet-safe products, bring our own water and power backup where needed, and price the work before we start so there are no surprises at the end.
